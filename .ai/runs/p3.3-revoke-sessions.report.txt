## P3.3 — session revocation as a first-class governed action

### What was delivered

`akira.user.revoke_sessions@1` is now a governed capability of its own: it increments `token_version` while leaving `role` and `is_active` byte-identical, is audited with the standard envelope, takes an idempotency key, and refuses self-revocation against the acting identity.

### Order of operations (the safety invariant)

1. **Seeded first** — added the capability id to the existing `cacSeedGovernancePolicies()` loop (`helpers.php:298`), which already produces the exact neighbour tier.
2. **Verified a row exists** — before touching the route, I seeded and read the row back. Result showed `akira.user.revoke_sessions@1` with `caller_module=cms-akira-shell,cms-akira-core`, `allowed_roles=admin,administrator,superadmin`, `requires_protocol=v2`, `provider_activation_required=1`, `is_active=1` — equal to `akira.user.set_active@1`.
3. **Verified the live tenant** — tenant 54 (`akira`) already carries an **active** policy row for the new capability at the same tier as the active `set_active@1` row:
   - `revoke_sessions@1` → `caller_module=cms-akira-shell,cms-akira-core`, `allowed_roles=admin,administrator,superadmin`, `requires_protocol=v2`, `provider_activation_required=1`, `is_active=1`
4. **Only then declared** the route in `routes.php` and `module.json`.
