## Two findings you should know about

**1. I deliberately did not use `capabilities.routes`.** The contract's item 3/4 cannot coexist with its own acceptance criteria here:
- Anonymous dispatch fails with `unknown_role` when `actor_role === ''` (`CapabilityAuthorizationRegistry.php:78`) — so declaring the route would 403 **every crawler regardless of a seeded policy row**. A newly-created governed capability likewise cannot be *called* from the anonymous handler (`CapabilityBus::applyAuthorizationRegistry()` throws).
- Adding the two GET declarations breaks the uneditable `tests/read_authority_probe_test.php`, which asserts the **exact** declared-GET set and lists public surfaces as non-declarable (`:142,176,184-193`); that test lives under forbidden `tests/`.

So both routes carry **reasoned `governance.exemptions`** — the repo's established shape for public routes (precedent: theme health). Existing `entity.list.post@1` already covers the published syndication read, so no new capability or policy row was needed; `cacActivePolicyVersion()` was therefore not applicable and no literal `policy_version` was pinned.

**2. The kernel page cache was serving the sitemap as `text/html`.** `pageCacheServe()` hardcodes `Content-Type: text/html` on a hit (`src/helpers/page-cache.php:334`) and doesn't persist headers. The first live response was correct; a repeat became `text/html`. Since `src/` is forbidden and `config/` is outside the 5-file scope, I added a narrow, documented opt-out in `helpers.php` using the cache's own `?nocache=1` bypass — for exactly the two paths. Verified: sitemap/robots keep their types on repeats, while `/` and `/posts` still show `X-Page-Cache: hit`.
