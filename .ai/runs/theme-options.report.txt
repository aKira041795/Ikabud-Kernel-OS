### Control support found

Before this change, Theme Studio rendered:

- `color` as `<input type="color">`
- `number` as `<input type="number">`
- everything else as free text

The declarative provider supports `boolean`, `number`, `integer`, `color`, `select`, and `textarea`. Theme Studio now also renders real `<select>` controls and applies `min`, `max`, and `step` attributes to numeric inputs.

Numeric values are additionally validated server-side. Malformed, non-finite, or out-of-range values produce **HTTP 422**, and the customization transaction stores nothing.
