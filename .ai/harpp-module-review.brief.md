   divergence as a concrete `file:line` item with the smallest correction it needs.
5. **Will the templates compile?** Run the repository's own DiSyL linter over every `.disyl` in
   `templates/modules/harpp` (`php _lint_disyl.php <file>`) and report the real results. Note the standing rule: if

7. **Security review.** `auth_owned` bootstrap password hashes, the PWA surface (`sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`),

8. **What would activation cost?** The ordered list of work to make this module live on a tenant here: migrations,
   seeding, policy rows (it exposes 36 capabilities), settings defaults, templates, assets, and the bridge coupling.
   Estimate each item in one line. Do **not** perform any of it.
9. **Recommendation.** One of: *activate as-is* · *activate after the listed corrections* · *do not activate, and
   here is what to salvage instead*. Name the single biggest risk either way.
