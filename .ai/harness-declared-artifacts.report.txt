The design in one line: a declared harness artefact is a PRE-COMMITMENT made at `start`, recorded in
the run record as `harness_artifacts`, shown in `scope_conformance.declared_harness_artifacts`, and
removed only from what is ATTRIBUTED to the executor. It is never a blanket exemption: GUARD 1
refuses a verifier or forbidden_scope declaration, GUARD 2 refuses a finish declaration, and an
undeclared out-of-scope path still blocks exactly as it did before.
